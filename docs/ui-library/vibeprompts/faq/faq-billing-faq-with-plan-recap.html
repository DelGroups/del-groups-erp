Billing questions

If something here does not match your invoice, reply to it and a human will look.

Payments

When is my card actually charged?

It depends on the plan shape. Charges are attempted at 09:00 UTC and retried twice over four days if they fail.

MonthlySame date each month, in advance
AnnualOn the anniversary, in advance
UsageFirst working day of the following month
Can we pay by invoice and bank transfer?

From $5,000 annually, yes — net 30 as standard and net 60 on request. Below that we only take cards, because chasing small invoices costs more than the invoices.

Do you charge VAT?

We charge VAT where required. Add a valid EU VAT number in billing settings and the reverse charge is applied to the next invoice automatically.

Cancellation

What happens to our data if we cancel?

The workspace goes read-only at the end of the paid period and stays that way for 60 days, so exports still work. After that it is deleted, including backups, within a further 30 days.

Do you give refunds?

On annual plans, pro rata for whole unused months. On monthly plans we do not, but you can cancel any time and keep access until the period ends.
